Put anthracite essential normal text on Signal Orange — it measures 6.43 — and never white, which measures 2.87 and would not read.

Observable No white text is written on Signal Orange anywhere in the system's own surfaces.

Checked by Render every surface that carries the signal and read the text against it.

COL-003MUSTcolor

Declare quantity, range, neutral point if applicable, endpoints, missing state and legend for a scalar field.

Observable The field can be interpreted without treating a gradient as ornament.

Checked by Inspect field annotation and accessible description.

COL-004MUSTcolor

A product may carry its own content palette for what it is showing — a diagram, a chart, a data visualisation, a category of thing — provided it is declared and provided it does not carry the interface. The interface is the system's; the content is the product's.

Observable The colours a product declares as content are named in one place with a reason, and every colour in the interface is one of the system's.

Checked by The consumer check reports the colours a consumer carries that the system does not have, and a consumer that has declared a content palette is absorbed when what is left is nothing.

COL-005MUSTcolor

A scheme that changes the ink changes the ground the ink is written on. The system's grounds are not dark enough to carry every ink, so a scheme carries the grounds its own ink needs.

Observable Run python3 tools/audit_scheme.py against the scheme: every pairing the system states must still read, on both substrates.

Checked by The contract's own two schemes pass the audit, and a consumer's scheme passes it before the scheme is applied.

COL-006MUSTcolor

A role that carries text is measured against the ground it is written on, and the pairing measures at least 4.5:1. A role that carries a mark measures at least 3:1, and the ground where it does not reach it is excluded from the role.

Observable Every pairing in the contrast table measures 4.5:1 or more, and every role that carries text wears a colour the table measures against a ground.

Checked by Read the contrast table and the role layer: every text role's colour is measured against the ground it sits on.

ICON-001MUSTicons

Draw interface glyphs from the ratified Tabler subset in assets/icons/. Do not hand-author an SVG glyph, borrow a glyph from another set, or substitute a text character for an icon.

Observable Every interface glyph in a consumer surface resolves to a file listed in assets/icons/icon-manifest.json, at the upstream digest recorded there. Country and organisation flags resolve to the ratified Tabler Flags subset.

Checked by Run tools/vendor_icons.py --check and confirm no consumer surface contains a hand-authored <svg> glyph or a Unicode character standing in for an icon.

ID-003MUST NOTidentity

Composite a ratified alpha identity master over a chromatic substrate. Alpha masters are sanctioned on neutral substrates only.

NAM-001MUSTnaming

A published token name is the layer id, kebab-cased, under the --gnosix- prefix. No projection may rename a layer id, and no consumer may write a name the system does not publish.

Observable The set of --gnosix-* names in the published web projection equals the set derived from the layer ids in core/TOKENS.json and core/SURFACES.json by the one published function.

Checked by The naming gate derives the expected name set from the contract and compares it with the published projection. The rule was moved out of the projection and into the contract because a vendored copy of the namespace drifted in spelling while every projection still agreed with itself.

STA-001MUST NOTstate

SUR-001MUSTsurfaces

Keep geometry, material, chrome, depth, content, input geometry and allocation independently representable where applicable.

Observable Material and shape may vary without changing semantic role.

Checked by Compare surface fixture variants and target mappings.

SUR-002MUSTsurfaces

Compose first, paint once. A surface is composed into one canonical boundary before it is filled or stroked: semantic regions compose into a single material boundary, and the surface is painted by one path and one owner.

Observable No independently stroked shell region, no duplicate shell stroke and no profile-private deformation survives a settled surface.

Checked by Compose the regions of a surface and compare the resulting boundary with the one that is painted: they are the same.

SUR-003MUSTsurfaces

The curvature of a shell is one authority for the whole composition. A region does not carry a radius of its own, and a window's rounding is the shell's rounding.

Observable A window's corner radius equals the shell's radius, and the exposed lower turns consume the same global curvature.

Checked by Read the radius of the shell and of every window on the output; they are one number.

SUR-004MUSTsurfaces

A settled surface is a canonical state rebuilt from the composition, not the residue of an animation. A transition has no independently renderable primitive, and a rebuild at rest equals a fresh build.

Observable The settled pose is byte-for-byte the pose a fresh build produces, and the transition contributes no layer of its own.

Checked by Settle a surface, rebuild it from the canonical state, and compare the two renders.

SUR-005MUSTsurfaces

A surface's occupancy is stated, never inferred from its dimensions, and each output resolves its own policy. A compositor's fullscreen is an explicit occupancy class.

Observable The occupancy class is present in the state that the compositor receives; no policy is chosen from a width or a height.

Checked by Compare the occupancy and the policy of two outputs of the same session; they are resolved independently.

SUR-006MUSTsurfaces

A configuration epoch isolates what a profile may not bleed into another. Private motion state does not cross an epoch.

Observable Applying a configuration to one output does not change the state of another, and a profile's private state does not survive its epoch.

Checked by Apply a configuration on one output and read the other; it is unchanged.

SUR-007MUSTsurfaces

A surface's axes are ordered. One axis settles before the next begins, and a settled axis carries no residue into the next.

Observable While one axis is moving the other holds its canonical value, and a settled surface's axes read zero, one or their canonical value — never a leftover.

Checked by Move one axis and read the other; it is at its canonical value.

SUR-008MUSTsurfaces

The topology of a surface is not its behaviour. A topology says how the material is arranged; the policies — the workarea, the fullscreen response, the overlap response — are stated beside it and are never inferred from it.

Observable Changing the topology of a profile changes how the material is arranged and nothing else: the resolved policies are the same.

Checked by Hold the policies and change the topology; the resolved policies do not move.

SUR-009MUSTsurfaces

A surface that a policy suppresses paints nothing, and no boundary of it remains in the surface.

Observable A suppressed surface has no painted boundary, and the surface it settles into is the one it would have reached had it never been suppressed.

Checked by Suppress a surface, settle it, and compare with a surface that was never suppressed.

SUR-010MUSTsurfaces

A surface's geometry is finite. No non-finite value reaches a path, an axis or a wave.

Observable Every number in a surface's state and in its path is finite.

Checked by Read the state and the path of a surface, and assert that no value is NaN or infinite.

SUR-011MUSTsurfaces

A scheme that does not know its values states the computation and the floor that computation guarantees. A derived role is read at its floor, not at a value.

Observable Every role a scheme derives names a derivation the contract holds, and that derivation's floor clears every minimum the pairing the role is read in requires.

Checked by Declare a scheme that derives an ink from an unknown ground and run tools/audit_scheme.py. A floor under the pairing's minimum fails, and a clamped derivation with no deviation fails.

TYP-001MUSTtypography

Use Space Grotesk as the primary family for interface and authored reference text; do not apply this rule to glyphs already baked into ratified identity artwork.

Observable Live interface/reference text resolves to Space Grotesk; cube symbols remain image content.

Checked by Inspect computed interface family and verify identity assets are referenced as images rather than reconstructed live text.

TYP-002MUSTtypography

Express technical or evidence text with the technical family — IBM Plex Mono — and, where the field says so, with Space Grotesk using weight, tracking and tabular numerals.

Observable Paths, IDs, measurements and evidence labels resolve to the technical family; numeric metadata may use tabular figures.

Checked by Search profile/reference styles for undeclared font-family overrides and inspect technical specimens.

The states

default

The state a thing is in when nothing has happened to it.

Role —

Treatment The element's own surface and its own type, and nothing else.

Evidence A capture of the resting surface, on both substrates.

hover

The pointer is over the thing.

Role —

Treatment A surface treatment that the pointer leaving takes back, and that never pretends to be focus.

Evidence A pointer capture, and a keyboard traversal that shows the two are different.

focus-visible

The keyboard is on the thing.

Role focus

Treatment An explicit visible focus treatment, independent of hover, and readable against the surface it sits on.

Evidence A keyboard traversal capture, and the contrast of the focus treatment against that surface.

active

The thing is being pressed.

Role —

Treatment A pressed surface, distinct from focus, and a pointer that says it.

Evidence A press capture, and the keyboard equivalent where the thing has one.

disabled

The thing cannot be acted on.

Role —

Treatment A surface that reads as unavailable without relying on colour alone, and a pointer that says so.

Evidence A capture of the disabled surface, and a pointer check.

busy

The thing is working, and the work is not finished.

Role —

Treatment A progress indication that expresses the wait and never resolves it: the state stays pending until an observation reports a result.

Evidence A pending capture, and a check that the pending state is not the observed one.

open

The thing is showing what it holds.

Role —

Treatment An overlay above the surface that opened it, which takes the keyboard while it is open.

Evidence A capture of the open state, and the keyboard traversal that shows where the keyboard went.

interactive

The thing can be acted on.

Role —

Treatment A hit region and a place in the focus order, so the thing is reachable without a pointer.

Evidence A keyboard traversal, and a pointer capture of the same region.

invalid

The thing holds something that is not acceptable.

Role critical

Treatment The critical treatment on the field, and the message that says why, in text.

Evidence A capture of the invalid state, and a check that the message is there.

on

The thing is switched on.

Role selection

Treatment The selection treatment on the surface and the edge, and a state a screen reader reads.

Evidence The selected-state semantics, and a capture of both states.
